About the doctor

After the first fracture visit

Start with the injury and the care you have received.

Dr. Harish Menon is an orthopedic surgeon at Harbour Fracture Clinic in Kochi. Your discharge notes help connect hospital care with the questions that come afterward. A visit can begin with dressing with a cast or organising the trip to a follow-up appointment. He asks about the date of injury, the initial treatment and the instructions you received. If you have previous imaging, bring the images as well as the written report. He considers them alongside your account of daily function and explains any findings in plain language. There is time to ask what can be assessed online and when a clinic examination is appropriate. Together you can discuss suitable options, what they involve and when the plan should be reviewed. At follow-up, an example from your own routine can help show what has changed since the first visit.
