About the doctor

A closer look at walking

Your footwear and walking route can help tell the story.

Dr. Mohit Bansal is an orthopedic surgeon at Footprint Orthopedics in Jaipur. The pair of shoes you wear most often can be a useful starting point. A visit can begin with a long shift on your feet or changing from shoes to sandals. He asks about where pressure is felt, which shoes you usually wear and your longest periods of standing. You can bring a list of questions, earlier reports and the names of any medicines or supports you use. He reviews these with you and explains which questions a physical examination could help answer. Together you can discuss suitable options, what they involve and when the plan should be reviewed. At follow-up, an example from your own routine can help show what has changed since the first visit.

Care philosophy

Consider the surface and the shoe

A walking history includes where you go, what you wear and how long you stand.

Hear the whole story

Tell the story in your own words, including what happens on an ordinary day.

Examine movement

Discuss where pressure is felt, which shoes you usually wear and your longest periods of standing alongside the examination and any existing reports.

Make space for questions

Agree a way to review progress that reflects your goals, such as a long shift on your feet or changing from shoes to sandals.
