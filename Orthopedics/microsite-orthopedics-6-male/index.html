About the doctor

Back symptoms in context

Tell the doctor how your back feels through the day.

Dr. Prateek Dutta is an orthopedic surgeon at Northline Spine Practice in Kolkata. A good history includes the breaks, positions and activities in an ordinary day. A visit can begin with sitting through a commute or standing at the kitchen counter. He asks about where symptoms travel, what changes with position and how sleep or work is affected. If you have previous imaging, bring the images as well as the written report. He considers them alongside your account of daily function and explains any findings in plain language. There is time to ask what can be assessed online and when a clinic examination is appropriate. Together you can discuss suitable options, what they involve and when the plan should be reviewed. At follow-up, an example from your own routine can help show what has changed since the first visit.
