Frequently Asked Questions

Practical information before your first consultation.

The first visit includes a detailed discussion of your main concern, symptom timeline, medical history, medicines, reports, sleep, appetite and routine. The doctor will also explain whether another medical assessment is needed.

You may discuss recurring or long term concerns, sleep and stress related symptoms, skin or allergy related concerns, and general follow-up questions. The consultation does not replace emergency care, diagnostic testing or specialist treatment.

Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency facility for severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, stroke signs, loss of consciousness, major bleeding, severe allergic reaction or any rapidly worsening condition. Do not wait for a website response.

Online consultations are governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. They are not for emergencies, and the doctor may advise an in-person visit where an examination is needed.
