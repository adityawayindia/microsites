Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first homeopathic consultation.

Dr. Rao commonly sees patients for skin and hair concerns, respiratory allergies, digestive complaints, joint and back pain, and stress- or sleep-related problems. The first consultation helps decide whether homeopathy is a suitable option for you.

The first visit usually takes 45 to 60 minutes, as it includes detailed case-taking. Follow-up visits are shorter, typically 15 to 20 minutes. No referral is needed to book.

Please bring any previous medical records or test reports, a list of current medicines, and details of your symptoms, lifestyle and health history so that case-taking can be thorough.

Yes. Online video consultations are available for follow-ups and for patients who cannot visit the clinic in person. Select the online option when booking.

Please tell the doctor about every medicine you take. Do not stop or change any prescribed medicine without speaking to the doctor who prescribed it. Homeopathy is not meant for emergencies, so for urgent symptoms go to the nearest hospital.

Online consultations are governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. They are not for emergencies, and the doctor may advise an in-person visit where an examination is needed.
