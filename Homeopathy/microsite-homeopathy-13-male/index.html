About the Physician

Careful Listening. Clear Guidance. Coordinated Care.

A measured consultation process that considers your history, current treatment and day-to-day health context.

Dr. Vihaan Kamat has practised since 2013 and conducts detailed consultations for adults, children and families. The first visit covers the main concern, previous diagnoses, current medicines, investigations, lifestyle, sleep and other factors that may influence the consultation plan.

He explains where homeopathy may be considered as a complementary option and where conventional medical evaluation, testing or referral is more appropriate. Patients are asked to continue prescribed medicines unless the original prescriber advises a change.

Follow-up visits focus on what has changed, what has not changed and whether the plan should continue, be adjusted or be referred onward. No outcome is guaranteed, and urgent, worsening or examination-dependent concerns are directed to suitable in-person care.

Care Philosophy

A Thoughtful Consultation, Without Overpromising

Every plan begins with context, respects existing care and stays open to reassessment.

Listen Before Prescribing

Allow enough time to understand the concern, previous treatment and the patient’s priorities.

Coordinate With Existing Care

Review current medicines and reports, and avoid asking patients to discontinue prescribed treatment without their prescriber.

Review, Explain, Refer

Document progress, explain next steps and recommend conventional assessment or urgent care when the situation calls for it.
