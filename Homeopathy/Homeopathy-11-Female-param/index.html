Practical answers before your first homeopathy consultation.

The first visit includes a detailed discussion of your main concern, medical history, current medicines, routines, sleep, stress and relevant reports. Additional time is allowed so the history can be reviewed carefully.

Bring recent prescriptions, reports, scans, allergy information and a current list of medicines or supplements. For a child, a parent or lawful guardian should attend and bring relevant vaccination and growth records.

Patients may discuss recurring skin, allergy, digestive, sleep, stress and respiratory symptoms, along with general wellbeing questions. The consultation determines whether homeopathy, testing, referral or another care pathway is appropriate.
