About the Specialist

Thoughtful Case-Taking. Individualised Remedies. Steady Follow-Up.

Homeopathic consultations in Baner, Pune and online, for adults and families managing long-standing health concerns.

Dr. Siddharth Rao is a homeopathic physician who has practised in Pune since 2010. He completed his BHMS and MD (Homeopathy) and holds a postgraduate diploma in hospital and healthcare management. His clinical work centres on skin conditions, respiratory allergies, digestive complaints, joint pain and lifestyle-related concerns in working adults.

Dr. Rao believes a good prescription starts with listening. He takes time to understand each patient's symptoms alongside their work routine, sleep, diet, stress and family history, and uses a structured repertory analysis to select an individualised remedy. He explains the plan in plain language so patients know what to expect between visits.

He works alongside a patient's existing medical care rather than replacing it, and encourages patients to keep their regular doctors informed. Follow-up reviews are planned at clear intervals, and the remedy, dose or advice is adjusted as the patient's health changes.
