About the Specialist

Listening First. Planning Carefully. Reviewing Progress.

Detailed homeopathy consultations for adults and children, with clear follow up and referral guidance when another form of care is needed.

Dr. Evelyn Thorne is a homeopathy physician practising since 2010. Her consultations begin with a careful review of symptoms, medical history, current medicines, routines, sleep, stress and the patient’s priorities.

She explains the proposed homeopathic plan, the expected follow up, and the limits of the approach in clear language. Patients are asked to continue prescribed medicines unless the clinician who prescribed them advises a change.

Clinic and online consultations are available. Emergencies and symptoms that require examination, testing or urgent treatment are directed to appropriate medical services.
