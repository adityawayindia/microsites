About the Specialist

Gut health. Advanced diagnostics. Compassionate healing.

Dr. Sarah Jenkins is a board-certified Gastroenterologist with over 12 years of experience in diagnosing and treating complex digestive disorders, liver conditions, and inflammatory bowel diseases. She specializes in advanced diagnostic endoscopy, colonoscopy, and personalized gut health therapies.

Recognized for her patient-centered approach and advanced clinical expertise, Dr. Jenkins works closely with multidisciplinary medical teams to provide evidence-based care tailored to each patient's lifestyle and wellness goals.
