About the Specialist

Gut health. Advanced diagnostics. Compassionate healing.

Dr. Arjun Mehta is a gastroenterologist with over a decade of experience in diagnosing and treating disorders of the digestive system and liver. He trained at AIIMS New Delhi and specialises in advanced endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease, fatty liver and chronic liver conditions. His approach combines accurate diagnostics with evidence-based therapy and practical dietary guidance, so that every patient understands their condition and their treatment plan. He believes in patient education, early screening and long-term follow-up, and consults patients both in clinic and online.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first gastroenterology consultation.

Blood in stool, a lasting change in bowel habit, unexplained weight loss and iron deficiency anaemia can be signs. Screening from age 45 detects it early when cure is most likely.

Raised enzymes on a liver function test show liver irritation from fatty liver, viruses, alcohol or medicines. Finding the cause is important, and levels often improve once it is treated.

There is little evidence that detox diets help, and extreme versions can cause weakness and imbalance. A balanced diet with fibre, fluids and regular meals supports the gut better.

Part of the stomach slips up through the diaphragm, causing reflux, belching and chest discomfort. Lifestyle changes and medicines control most cases, and surgery is rarely needed.

Acidity, IBS, ulcers, gallstones and food intolerances can all cause recurrent pain. Where and when the pain occurs guides the tests needed.

It is a small tear in the anal lining causing sharp pain and a little bright red blood during stools. Fibre, water, warm sitz baths and prescribed ointments heal most fissures.
