About the doctor

Authentic healing. Lasting wellness.

Personalized Ayurvedic consultations and therapies designed to restore balance and promote natural health.

Dr. Arjun Mehta is an Ayurvedic physician with over 12 years of experience in clinical practice, Panchakarma therapies, and constitutional (Prakriti) analysis. Chronic disease management, lifestyle modifications, and natural herb-based care plans tailored to each individual form the core of the practice. Recognized for a thorough approach and clear treatment plans, Dr. Sharma helps patients manage long-term health concerns, ranging from metabolic disorders to musculoskeletal pain, with systematic, evidence-based Ayurvedic practices.
