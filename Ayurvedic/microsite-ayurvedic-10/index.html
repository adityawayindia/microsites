About the doctor

Care that considers
your daily life.

An Ayurvedic perspective with a clear focus on your concerns, context and next steps.

Dr. Kavya Menon takes time to understand the concerns that bring each person to the clinic. An Ayurvedic consultation considers the health history, daily routine, existing reports and current medicines before discussing the next steps. Questions are welcome throughout the visit. The approach is to explain the assessment in clear language, discuss the suitability of any proposed care and agree on a follow-up plan. Where a concern calls for further investigation or another specialist, this is discussed as part of the consultation. The practice offers clinic appointments and online follow-ups where appropriate, with the same attention to listening and continuity.
