The person behind the care

A considered approach to cardiology.

Time to listen. Space to understand.

Dr. Aarav Mehta is an illustrative consultant cardiologist whose practice brings together preventive cardiology, blood pressure management and the assessment of heart-related symptoms. His approach begins with listening: understanding what has changed, reviewing the medical history and making space for the questions that matter to each patient. He discusses test results in plain language and explains the options for further evaluation. Appointments may include a review of current medicines, earlier ECGs, echocardiograms and blood tests. The aim is a clear conversation about the next step, with follow-up shaped around the individual. Clinic visits allow an in-person assessment, while an online consultation can help with suitable follow-up and report review.
