About the doctor

A considered approach
to dental care.

Dental consultations with room for questions, context and continuity.

Dr. Rohan Mehta offers dental consultations with a focus on listening, careful assessment and clear explanations. The first conversation covers your concerns, dental history, current medicines and any previous treatment. An examination helps identify what needs attention and whether further assessment is appropriate. The dentist explains the findings, discusses suitable options and makes time for questions about the proposed care. Treatment is planned around individual needs and informed consent, with referrals discussed when specialist care is needed. Follow-up visits provide an opportunity to review comfort, healing and ongoing oral care. Clinic appointments and online discussions are available when enabled by the practice; an in-person visit may be needed for examination or treatment.
