Braces vs Clear Aligners: Which Is Right for You

Dr. Rohan Kapoor

Orthodontist & Dentist

Updated on 12 Jun 2026

Both braces and clear aligners can correct the same range of bite and spacing issues, but they differ in how they look, feel, and fit into daily life—so the right choice usually comes down to your case and your routine, not just preference.

What sets them apart

Metal or ceramic braces stay fixed on the teeth and work well for complex movements that need constant, precise force. Clear aligners are removable, near-invisible trays best suited to mild-to-moderate cases where discretion and easy cleaning matter.

Aligners only work if they're worn as prescribed—usually 20 to 22 hours a day. Braces keep working around the clock without relying on patient compliance.

How we decide together

A digital scan and bite assessment tell us how much movement your case actually needs. From there, we walk through timeline, cost, and lifestyle trade-offs so the decision is yours to make with full information, not a default recommendation.

If you're weighing braces against aligners, a consultation with imaging is the fastest way to get a real answer instead of a guess.
