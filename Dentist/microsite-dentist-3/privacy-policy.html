This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use this website and its consultation and appointment services.

1) Who We Are

This is the personal microsite of Dr. Rohan Iyer, BDS, MDS (Prosthodontics), Registration No. 58216. It is hosted on technology provided by DigiDr (digidr.app).

Dr. Rohan Iyer decides why and how your details are used for your consultation and is the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). DigiDr provides the technology and handles your details only on the doctor's instructions. DigiDr is not a healthcare provider.

2) Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details (name, email, phone number)
  • Appointment details (preferred date and time, reason for visit)
  • Documents you upload through forms (for example, reports and prescriptions)
  • Website usage data (browser type, pages viewed, device and basic analytics)

Please share only what is needed for your visit. Do not upload government ID numbers or payment card details through the forms.

3) How We Use Your Information, and Your Consent

Your information is used to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Respond to inquiries and provide support
  • Send messages about your appointment
  • Improve the website using anonymous, combined usage data
  • Meet legal, medical, and regulatory obligations

When you submit a booking or inquiry form, you consent to your details being used to arrange and provide your consultation. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect use made before you withdrew, and we may keep records that the law requires. We send no promotional messages without your separate consent. Your submitted health details are never used to improve the website.

4) Medical Privacy

We treat all health-related information with strict confidentiality. Any data shared through this website is handled with appropriate safeguards and is accessed only by authorised personnel as needed for care, administration, or legal compliance. DigiDr personnel can access it only where needed to run, secure, or support the service.

5) AI-Assisted Content

Where health education on this site is drafted with AI, it is reviewed and approved by Dr. Rohan Iyer before publication and labelled as AI-assisted. Details you submit in forms or documents are not used to create public content.

6) Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. We may share information only when necessary with:

  • Authorised staff and healthcare professionals involved in your care
  • Trusted service providers, including DigiDr as the technology provider, and hosting, appointment, or communication tools
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law

Where a service provider stores data outside India, this is done in line with the DPDP Act.

7) Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as required to provide services, maintain records, and comply with applicable laws and medical record policies. When data is no longer needed and no law requires us to keep it, it is deleted or made anonymous.

8) Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. However, no website or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9) Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand usage patterns and improve functionality. You can change your browser settings to control cookie preferences.

10) Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update your data
  • Ask for your data to be erased
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Have your grievance addressed
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act

To use any of these rights, write to Dr. Rohan Iyer using the details below, or to DigiDr at info@digidr.app. If your grievance is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11) Children

If the patient is under 18, the booking must be made by a parent or lawful guardian, who gives consent for the child. We do not track or target children with advertising.

12) Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites and recommend reviewing their policies separately.

13) Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date. For changes that affect how your details are used, we will tell you where we hold your contact details.
