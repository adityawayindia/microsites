About the Practice

Gentle Dentistry, Built Around Your Smile

Calm, unhurried dental care—clear explanations, careful diagnosis and comfortable treatment for every age.

Dr. Rohan Iyer is a dental surgeon focused on comfortable, long-lasting care—from routine check-ups and root canal treatment to dental implants, crowns and smile makeovers—for patients of every age. The practice is built on a prevention-first philosophy: a thorough examination and digital X-rays come before any treatment plan, so every recommendation is clear, justified and explained in plain language.

Fellowship-trained in advanced implantology and aesthetic dentistry, Dr. Iyer favours minimally invasive, tooth-preserving techniques that keep procedures gentle and recovery short. Appointments are unhurried, with every option, cost and timeline laid out before a decision is made—and a follow-up plan that helps keep your smile healthy for years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first visit.

The practice treats the full range of everyday dental needs, including toothache, cavities, gum problems, root canal treatment, missing teeth, crooked teeth and cosmetic concerns, for children as well as adults.

No referral is required. You can book a clinic visit or an online consultation directly through this website, and the clinic will guide you on any further steps after your first appointment.

Please bring any previous dental X-rays or reports, a valid ID, and a list of medicines you are currently taking and any allergies, so your dental and medical history can be reviewed thoroughly.

Modern dentistry is designed to be as comfortable as possible. Local anaesthesia and gentle techniques are used where needed, and you can ask for a break at any point. Mention any anxiety when you book so the visit can be planned around it.

Yes. Online consultations are available for follow-ups, report reviews and initial advice. Select “Online Consultation” when booking. Treatments such as fillings, root canals or implants need an in-person visit.

For severe pain, facial swelling, heavy bleeding or an injury to the face or jaw, please visit the nearest emergency room immediately. For other urgent concerns, book the earliest available slot and the clinic will prioritise your case.

Online consultations are governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. They are not for emergencies, and the doctor may advise an in-person visit where an examination is needed.
