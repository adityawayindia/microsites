About the Practice

Whole-Person Care, Built Around Your Family

One trusted doctor for everyday illness, long-term conditions and prevention—with time to listen and clear answers in plain language.

Dr. Karan Malhotra is a general physician focused on adult and family medicine—from fevers, infections and seasonal illness to diabetes, thyroid disorders, blood pressure and other long-term conditions. The practice is built on a listen-first philosophy: a detailed history and careful examination come before any test or prescription, so every recommendation is justified, proportionate and explained in plain language.

Fellowship-trained in diabetology and preventive medicine, Dr. Malhotra places equal weight on staying well and on getting well. Regular screening, vaccination advice and practical guidance on diet, activity and sleep sit alongside treatment, and patients are referred promptly to the right specialist whenever a problem needs more than primary care. Consultations are unhurried, with every option laid out before a decision is made and a clear follow-up plan afterwards.
