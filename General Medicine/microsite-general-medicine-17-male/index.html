About the doctor

What's worth checking at this stage of life?

A preventive visit starts with your age, health history and priorities for the years ahead.

Dr. Prithvi Saikia is an internal medicine physician at Cedar Gate Medicine in Guwahati. He asks about family medical history, previous screening, vaccinations and changes in everyday life. Bring the reports and records you have, particularly those that show when a check was last performed. Screening decisions take account of personal risk, previous findings and whether a result would change care. There is space to ask which checks can wait and what you can do between appointments. Symptoms that are already bothering you deserve a separate discussion as part of the visit.

The virus inflames the joints, so pain and stiffness can last weeks or months after the fever settles. Rest, gentle movement and doctor advised pain relief help recovery.

Wheezing, a tight chest, night cough and breathlessness with dust or cold air point to asthma. Inhalers control it well and let people live fully active lives.

Tiredness that lasts weeks, comes with weight change, pallor, breathlessness or low mood needs checking. Common causes include anaemia, thyroid problems, vitamin deficiency and diabetes.
