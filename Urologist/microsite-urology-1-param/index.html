Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first visit.

We diagnose and treat the full range of urological conditions, including kidney stones, prostate disorders, urinary tract infections, incontinence, and male infertility, using the latest minimally invasive techniques.

No referral is required. You can book a clinic visit or an online consultation directly through this website, and we'll guide you on any further steps after your first appointment.

Please bring any prior test reports, scans, or prescriptions related to your condition, a valid ID, and a list of medications you're currently taking so we can review your history thoroughly.
