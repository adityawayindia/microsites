Everyday Habits That Lower Your Risk Of Kidney Stones

Dr. Arjun Mehta

Consultant Urologist

Updated on 20 Apr 2026

Kidney stones are one of the most common urological conditions, but preventing them does not require complicated routines. Small, consistent daily habits can significantly lower your risk and support long-term kidney health.

1) Stay Consistently Hydrated Throughout The Day

Aim for at least 2.5–3 litres of water daily, more in hot weather or after exercise. Adequate fluid intake dilutes urine and prevents the mineral concentration that leads to stone formation.

2) Moderate Salt And Animal Protein Intake

Excess sodium and high animal protein intake increase calcium and uric acid in the urine. Reduce processed foods and balance meals with vegetables, fruits, and moderate portions of lean protein.

3) Don't Cut Out Dietary Calcium

Contrary to common belief, adequate dietary calcium from food (not supplements) actually helps bind oxalate in the gut and reduces stone risk. Avoid unnecessary calcium restriction without medical advice.

4) Track Symptoms And Get Timely Imaging

Flank pain, blood in urine, or recurrent UTIs should prompt a urology evaluation. Sharing your symptom history and any past stone episodes helps identify the exact stone type and guides targeted prevention.

When To Seek Urgent Urological Advice

Consult your urologist if pain is severe or persists beyond a few hours, or seek urgent care for warning signs such as fever with flank pain, inability to pass urine, persistent vomiting, or visible blood in the urine.
