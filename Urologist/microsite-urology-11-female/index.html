About the doctor

Discussing a procedure in detail

An informed decision begins with a discussion about the reason for treatment and what recovery might involve.

Dr. Tara Narayanan reviews urological concerns where minimally invasive procedures are being considered. She discusses reports with patients, makes time for questions about alternatives and plans follow-up around the care they need. Patients can bring a list of medicines, previous reports and questions about their next steps.
