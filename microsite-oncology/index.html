About the doctor

Trusted Medical Oncology Expertise

  • 18+ years in medical oncology
  • Multidisciplinary cancer care planning
  • Patient-first communication & support

Compassionate oncology care guided by experience, clarity, and hope.

Dr. Ananya Mehta is a leading medical oncologist with over 18 years of experience in diagnosing and treating solid tumors and hematologic malignancies. She combines evidence-based protocols with a patient-first approach, helping individuals and families navigate every stage of cancer care—from diagnosis and chemotherapy to immunotherapy, targeted therapy, and survivorship support.

Known for clear communication and careful treatment planning, Dr. Mehta works closely with surgical and radiation oncology teams to deliver coordinated, personalized care in a calm and supportive clinical environment.
