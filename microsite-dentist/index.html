About the doctor

Healthy teeth. Confident smiles.

Preventive, restorative, and aesthetic dental care designed to feel calm, clear, and comfortable.

Dr. Anika Sharma is a dentist with over 12 years of experience in preventive dentistry, restorative care, root canal treatment, and smile aesthetics. She focuses on comfortable visits, modern techniques, and practical guidance patients can follow at home.

Known for a calm chairside manner and transparent treatment plans, Dr. Sharma helps families and working professionals maintain healthy teeth—from routine cleanings through complex restorative work—with confidence and clarity.
