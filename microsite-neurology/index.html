About Dr. Smith

Trusted Neurology Expertise In Orlando

Combining over two decades of clinical experience with compassionate, patient‑first care.

Dr. Sarah Smith is a board-certified neurologist specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of complex brain, spine, and nerve conditions, including stroke, epilepsy, migraine disorders, movement disorders, and neuropathies. She is known for her calm, reassuring approach in high‑stress situations. Trained at leading institutions in the United States and abroad, Dr. Smith blends evidence‑based medicine with clear, honest communication. She takes time to explain every step of a patient’s care plan, ensuring you and your family feel confident and informed about your neurological health. Beyond acute care, Dr. Smith is passionate about preventive neurology—helping patients reduce stroke risk, manage chronic headaches, improve sleep, and address memory concerns early. Many families trust her as their long‑term partner for brain and nervous system health across generations.
