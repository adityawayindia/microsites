Finding the right comparison

The most relevant comparison may be an older examination rather than the most recent one.

Dr. Saira Mirza is a radiologist at Interval Imaging Practice in Lucknow. A recommendation for follow-up usually refers to a particular finding and a particular type of study. Bring the original recommendation as well as the new report and images. The radiologist can review which dates were compared, what changed in the technique and how the conclusion should be discussed with your treating clinician. She can go through the report in the order that makes sense to you. For example, you might want to start with its conclusion, check what was compared and then discuss the details that prompted the recommendation. You can bring a short list of questions and agree what to take back to the clinician who requested the examination.
