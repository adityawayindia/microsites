About the Practice

Imaging That Answers the Clinical Question

Safe, accurate scans with clear reports you can understand.

Dr. Vikram Rao is a consultant radiologist working across MRI, CT, digital X-ray, ultrasound and mammography, with a subspecialty focus on cross-sectional neuro, musculoskeletal and abdominal imaging. Every request is vetted before the patient is scanned: the protocol is matched to the clinical question, sequences are tailored rather than run from a default list, and contrast is used only where it will change the answer.

Imaging Services

A Full Diagnostic Imaging Suite

Every major modality under one roof, protocolled by a consultant and reported clearly.

MRI & Neuroimaging

High-field MRI of the brain, spine, joints and abdomen, with sequences tailored to the referring question rather than a default protocol list.

CT & CT Angiography

Multi-slice CT, contrast studies and vascular angiography acquired at the lowest diagnostic dose using dose-modulated protocols.

Digital X-Ray & Fluoroscopy

Digital radiography and fluoroscopic studies, including barium and contrast series, with instant image review.

Ultrasound & Doppler

Abdominal, obstetric, small-parts and colour Doppler studies performed and reported by the consultant at the console.

Interventional Radiology

Image-guided biopsies, drainages, aspirations and pain-management injections carried out under ultrasound or CT guidance.

Screening & Second Opinions

Mammography, whole-body screening packages and independent second-opinion reporting on studies acquired elsewhere.

Imaging Philosophy

The Right Study, Read the Right Way

Committed to vetted protocols, the lowest safe radiation dose, and reports that read clearly.

01

Protocol-Led Acquisition

Each request is vetted against the clinical question, so the study performed is the one that will actually answer it.

02

Lowest Safe Dose

Dose-modulated CT, non-ionising alternatives wherever they suffice, and strict paediatric weight-based protocols.

03

Reports You Can Act On

Structured reporting with a plain-language impression, key images flagged, and a direct call for urgent findings.

Scan Journey

From Referral to Report

A clear 4-step pathway, from booking your slot to talking your results through.

01

Booking & Preparation

Book online or by phone. You receive fasting, contrast and metal-safety instructions before you arrive.

02

Protocol Review

Your request and history are vetted by the consultant, and the sequences are set to your clinical question.

03

Scan Acquisition

The study is acquired at the lowest safe dose, with images checked for quality before you leave the suite.

04

Reporting & Follow-Up

A structured report, shared digitally, with a sit-down review of your images on request.
