About the Surgeon

Surgical Precision With an Artist's Eye

Every procedure is planned around your anatomy, your goals, and results that look like you — only refreshed.

Dr. Vikram Malhotra is a board-certified plastic, aesthetic, and reconstructive surgeon with over 15 years of experience in facial surgery, body contouring, and reconstructive procedures. After completing his MCh in Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery, he trained further in advanced rhinoplasty and aesthetic surgery, and today performs a wide range of cosmetic and restorative procedures for men and women.

His approach begins with listening. Every consultation includes a detailed facial or body analysis, an honest discussion of what surgery can and cannot achieve, and a plan tailored to your proportions rather than a one-size-fits-all template. Dr. Malhotra is known for subtle, natural-looking results — outcomes that restore confidence without announcing that surgery took place.

Beyond aesthetics, he has a strong reconstructive practice covering post-burn and post-trauma reconstruction, scar revision, and hand surgery. Patient safety comes first: procedures are performed in accredited operating theatres with experienced anaesthesia teams, and every patient receives a clear, structured recovery plan with close follow-up.

Procedures

Aesthetic & Reconstructive Procedures

Surgical and non-surgical treatments for the face, body, and skin — each planned for balanced, natural results.

Rhinoplasty

Nose reshaping for improved balance and breathing, including open, closed, and revision rhinoplasty.

Facial Rejuvenation

Facelift, neck lift, eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty), and chin or jawline contouring for a refreshed look.

Liposuction & Body Contouring

Targeted fat removal and sculpting of the abdomen, flanks, arms, and thighs, including tummy tuck.

Gynecomastia Surgery

Male chest reduction combining liposuction and gland excision for a flatter, firmer, more masculine contour.

Hair Restoration

FUE and FUT hair transplantation with natural hairline design, plus beard and eyebrow restoration.

Reconstructive & Scar Surgery

Scar revision, post-burn and post-trauma reconstruction, cleft care, and hand surgery to restore form and function.
