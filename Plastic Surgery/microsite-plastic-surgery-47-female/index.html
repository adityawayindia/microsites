About the Surgeon

Time to consider your options

The first visit gives you room to describe your priorities and ask about the practical details of treatment.

At the first visit, Dr. Naina Lall asks about your medical history, earlier procedures and the changes that matter to you. If you have consultation letters or photographs from earlier treatment, you can bring them along. The discussion includes how the concern affects comfort, movement and daily activities. Together you can decide which questions need an examination, what the alternatives are and how much time you would like before making a decision. At follow-up, you can revisit the plan, talk about healing and ask what to expect before returning to work or exercise.
