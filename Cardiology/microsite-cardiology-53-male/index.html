About the doctor

Meet your cardiologist

Cardiac imaging is part of a conversation about how your heart is working.

Dr. Devendra Menon is a consultant cardiologist in Kochi with a focus on echocardiography and valve disease. His consultations often begin with a question about a phrase in a report, a new heart murmur or a change in how an everyday activity feels. He reviews the images alongside the reason the test was requested and asks about earlier scans that might help with comparison. During a report review, patients can ask which measurements matter for their situation, which findings need observation and what would prompt another appointment. He encourages patients to bring the written report and earlier images when available, along with their current medicines and questions.

Your appointment

From referral to review

Four practical steps for an imaging-focused consultation.

  1. Choose a consultation

    Select a time for a clinic appointment or online report review.

  2. Gather your scans

    Bring the written reports and images from earlier examinations if available.

  3. Read the report together

    Discuss the question behind the test and the findings that relate to it.

  4. Discuss follow-up

    Check when to return and whether you need another test before that visit.
