Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first visit.

Children follow a national immunisation schedule from birth, covering diseases like polio, measles, hepatitis B and tetanus. Keep the vaccination card updated at every visit.

Keep the child hydrated, dress lightly and give age appropriate fever medicine. Seek care for fever in babies under 3 months, fits, rash, or if the child is unusually drowsy.

Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months, then iron rich and varied complementary foods. Limit sugary drinks and packaged snacks.

Dry mouth, no tears when crying, sunken eyes and fewer wet diapers are signs. Give ORS often and seek care if the child cannot keep fluids down.

Weight, height and milestone tracking against growth charts shows progress. Delays in speech, walking or social response should be discussed early.

Young children can get 6 to 8 colds a year, which is normal. Rest, fluids and saline drops help, and breathing difficulty needs prompt attention.

Online consultations are governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. They are not for emergencies, and the doctor may advise an in-person visit where an examination is needed.
