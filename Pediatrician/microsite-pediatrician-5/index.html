About the Practice

Gentle, Evidence-Based Pediatric Care

Combining rigorous clinical training with an unhurried, family-first approach to every consultation.

Dr. Rohan Kapoor is a board-certified pediatrician with over sixteen years of clinical experience spanning newborn, infant, child, and adolescent care. After completing his MBBS and MD in Pediatrics, he pursued a fellowship in Pediatric Pulmonology, deepening his expertise in childhood asthma, chronic cough, recurrent wheeze, and other respiratory conditions that are common yet often mismanaged in general practice. His clinical philosophy rests on one simple idea: every child deserves a calm, unhurried consultation where parents leave with clarity, not just a prescription.

Beyond respiratory care, Dr. Kapoor manages the full spectrum of general pediatrics — newborn checkups, growth and nutrition monitoring, vaccination schedules, fever and infection management, allergy care, and developmental screening. He is a firm believer in preventive medicine and works closely with families to keep vaccination calendars on track, catch growth concerns early, and build healthy daily habits that compound over a childhood.

Dr. Kapoor trained at premier medical institutions in India and completed additional clinical rotations focused on pediatric respiratory medicine. He continues to participate in continuing medical education, ensuring that every treatment plan he recommends reflects current, peer reviewed pediatric guidelines rather than outdated convention. Parents often note his patience in explaining diagnoses in plain language, and his willingness to answer every question — even the ones that feel too small to ask.

Outside the clinic, Dr. Kapoor mentors junior residents and volunteers with school health screening camps, believing that pediatric care extends beyond the four walls of a consultation room and into the everyday environments where children grow, play, and learn.
