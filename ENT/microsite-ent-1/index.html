About the Practice

Attentive, Complete Ear, Nose & Throat Care

Combining advanced surgical training with an unhurried, patient-first approach to every consultation.

Dr. Kabir Anand is a board-certified ENT specialist (otolaryngologist) specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of ear, nose, throat, and head-and-neck conditions in patients of every age. He manages hearing loss, chronic sinusitis, tonsil and adenoid disease, voice and swallowing disorders, and head-and-neck tumours with a calm, evidence-based approach. Trained at leading institutions in India and abroad, Dr. Anand blends surgical precision with clear communication for every patient.

He takes time to explain every diagnosis and treatment option, so patients feel informed and in control of their care. Beyond treatment, Dr. Anand is committed to minimally invasive endoscopic and microsurgical techniques that reduce recovery time—helping patients return to clear hearing, easy breathing, and a healthy voice faster, with less discomfort and better long-term outcomes.

Specialized Services

Comprehensive Ear, Nose & Throat Care

Offering a complete range of ENT healthcare services with personalized treatment and preventive guidance.

Hearing & Ear Care

Comprehensive hearing assessments, ear infection treatment, and micro-ear surgery for children and adults.

Sinus & Nasal Care

Diagnosis and treatment of chronic sinusitis, nasal blockage, deviated septum, and allergic rhinitis.

Throat & Voice Disorders

Evaluation and treatment of tonsillitis, laryngitis, voice disorders, and swallowing difficulties.

Minimally Invasive & Endoscopic Surgery

Endoscopic sinus surgery and microsurgical procedures for faster recovery and reduced surgical risk.

Sleep & Snoring Disorders

Diagnosis and treatment of snoring, sleep apnea, and airway obstruction for restful, healthy sleep.

Pediatric ENT & Allergy Care

Gentle evaluation and treatment for childhood ear infections, tonsils, adenoids, and nasal allergies.
