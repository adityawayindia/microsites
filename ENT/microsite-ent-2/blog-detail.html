Everyday Habits That Protect Your Hearing

Dr. Aarav Khanna

Consultant ENT Surgeon

Updated on 20 Apr 2026

Hearing loss is one of the most common ENT conditions, but protecting your hearing does not require complicated routines. Small, consistent daily habits can significantly lower your risk and support long-term hearing health.

1) Keep Noise Exposure Within Safe Limits

Limit prolonged exposure to sounds above 85 decibels, whether from headphones, traffic, or power tools. Lowering headphone volume and using ear protection in loud environments prevents the gradual damage that leads to noise-induced hearing loss.

2) Keep Ears Clean Without Cotton Buds

Inserting cotton buds or sharp objects into the ear canal pushes wax deeper and risks eardrum injury. Let ears self-clean naturally, and see an ENT specialist for safe wax removal if blockage causes discomfort or muffled hearing.

3) Don't Ignore Ringing Or Muffled Hearing

Contrary to common belief, tinnitus or muffled hearing after loud exposure is not always temporary. Persistent ringing can signal early hearing damage, so avoid dismissing it without a proper hearing evaluation.

4) Track Symptoms And Get Timely Hearing Tests

Ear pain, sudden hearing changes, or recurrent infections should prompt an ENT evaluation. Sharing your symptom history and any past ear episodes helps identify the exact cause and guides targeted treatment.

When To Seek Urgent ENT Advice

Consult your ENT specialist if pain is severe or persists beyond a few hours, or seek urgent care for warning signs such as sudden hearing loss, fluid or blood draining from the ear, severe dizziness, or facial weakness.
