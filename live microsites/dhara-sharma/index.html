Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding ear, nose, and throat treatments, consultations, and appointments.

Dr. Dhara Sharma treats conditions related to the ear, nose, and throat — including hearing loss, ear infections, sinus problems, nasal blockage, throat and voice disorders, tonsils, and snoring or sleep-related breathing issues.

You should consult her if you experience hearing difficulty, persistent ear pain or discharge, chronic sinus congestion, frequent nosebleeds, sore throat or voice changes lasting more than a few weeks, or snoring/sleep disturbances.

Yes, she provides ENT care for both children and adults, including pediatric ear infections, tonsil and adenoid issues, and nasal allergies in kids.

No referral is required. You can directly book a consultation for any ear, nose, or throat concern.

Please bring any previous medical records, hearing test reports, imaging/scan results (if available), current medications list, and a valid ID proof to help with an accurate diagnosis.
