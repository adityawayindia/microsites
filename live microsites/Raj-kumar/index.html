Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers for patients and families regarding critical care and emergency consultations.

Dr. Kumar Raj treats patients with serious, life-threatening conditions. As an Intensivist, he manages critically ill patients in the ICU — including ventilator support, monitoring, and organ support. As an Emergency Medicine specialist, he provides immediate, life-saving care for accidents, trauma, and sudden medical emergencies.

You should seek his care during medical emergencies such as chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, major trauma or accidents, sudden loss of consciousness, or severe infections — or if a loved one needs ICU-level critical care or post-operative monitoring.

Yes, Dr. Raj provides round-the-clock ICU and emergency care as Consultant and Head of the Emergency Medicine Department at ILS Hospital, Dumdum, Kolkata.

No, emergency cases should go directly to the hospital's emergency department. For scheduled consultations, follow-ups, or ICU-related discussions, you can book an appointment through this microsite.

Please bring previous medical records, current medications list, recent test reports (if any), and a valid ID proof to help Dr. Raj assess the condition quickly and accurately.
