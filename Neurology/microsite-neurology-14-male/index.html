About the doctor

Your first conversation

A witness account can help the neurologist understand an event you cannot fully recall.

Dr. Dhruv Soman is a neurologist at Orchard Neurology Clinic in Pune. He asks about what happened before, during and after an unexplained episode and makes time for the questions you want answered. The first visit brings together your account, earlier reports and a review of your medicines. If you are attending with someone, you can choose how they take part in the conversation. Where an examination is needed, the doctor explains what it involves and what information it may add. Before you leave, you can discuss what to record at home, how to share earlier findings and what you would like to revisit at the next appointment.

Areas of care

Consultations include seizure care, neurological assessment and follow-up visits.

Seizure and episode assessment

Headache consultation

Describe the timing and pattern of headaches and review how they affect your day.

Movement assessment

Discuss new tremor, stiffness, slowness or changes in walking and coordination.

Nerve symptoms

Map changes in sensation or strength and review earlier tests with the neurologist.

Stroke follow-up

Review hospital records and discuss recovery, medicines and rehabilitation goals.
