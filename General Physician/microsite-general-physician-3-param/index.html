Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most before their first visit.

A general physician is your first point of contact for most health concerns — fever and infections, diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid problems, allergies, stomach and breathing complaints, and long-term conditions. If you need a specialist, you will be referred to the right one.

No referral is required. You can book a clinic visit or an online consultation directly through this website, and we will guide you on any further steps after your first appointment.

Please bring any previous prescriptions, test reports or discharge summaries, a valid ID, and a list of the medicines you currently take so we can review your history thoroughly.

Yes. Online consultations are available for follow-ups, report reviews and initial assessments — simply select "Online Consultation" when booking your appointment.

For emergencies such as chest pain, severe breathlessness, sudden weakness or heavy bleeding, please go to the nearest emergency room immediately. For non-emergency concerns, book the earliest available slot and we will prioritise your case.

Online consultations are governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. They are not for emergencies, and the doctor may advise an in-person visit where an examination is needed.
