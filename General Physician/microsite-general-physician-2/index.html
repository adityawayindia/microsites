About the Physician

Comprehensive care. Preventive healing. Wellness for life.

Personalized health management, chronic condition control, and diagnostic excellence for your long-term wellness.

Dr. Sarah Jenkins is a General Physician with over 12 years of experience in diagnosing complex health conditions, managing chronic illnesses like diabetes and hypertension, and treating infectious diseases. Comprehensive health screenings, preventive care and lifestyle medicine form the core of the practice. Known for a thorough diagnostic approach and empathetic consultations, Dr. Sarah Jenkins works closely with patients and families to build personalized treatment plans that foster recovery and promote long-term well-being.
