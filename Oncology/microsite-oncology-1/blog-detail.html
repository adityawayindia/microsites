Understanding Early Cancer Symptoms

Dr. Ananya Mehta

Medical Oncologist

Updated on 12 Jun 2026

Early detection remains one of the most powerful tools in improving cancer outcomes. Many cancers show subtle warning signs long before they become advanced, and knowing what to look for can help patients seek care sooner.

Common warning signs

Persistent fatigue, unexplained weight loss, unusual lumps, changes in moles, lingering cough, and digestive changes should never be ignored. These symptoms do not always mean cancer, but they deserve timely medical evaluation.

When symptoms last longer than expected or keep returning, a specialist consultation can bring clarity and peace of mind.

Why screening matters

Age-appropriate screening for breast, cervical, colorectal, and lung cancers can identify disease at a treatable stage. Discuss your personal and family risk factors with your oncologist to build a screening plan that fits you.

If you have concerns about symptoms or want a second opinion, schedule a consultation with our oncology team. Clear answers and a thoughtful care plan start with a conversation.
