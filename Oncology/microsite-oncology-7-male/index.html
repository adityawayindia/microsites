About the doctor

Before starting chemotherapy

Use the consultation to ask how a proposed course might fit around meals, work and travel.

Dr. Kunal Pradhan is a medical oncologist at Linden Medical Oncology in Kolkata. A treatment name alone may leave a number of practical questions unanswered. The consultation can cover the reason chemotherapy is being considered, how a proposed course is organised and the support available between visits. Bring all prescribed medicines, supplements and allergies to the team's attention. He welcomes a written question list or a note from your referring doctor. You can ask for an explanation to be repeated and agree who will be in touch about the next step.

Helpful answers

Before your visit

Answers to common questions about your consultation and follow-up.

Unexplained weight loss, a lump, persistent cough, blood in stool or urine, and non healing sores are warning signs. Early detection greatly improves outcomes.

Chemotherapy uses medicines to destroy fast growing cancer cells. Nausea, hair loss and tiredness can occur, and supportive medicines reduce them.

It raises risk but does not guarantee cancer. Genetic counselling and earlier screening help those with a strong family history.

Surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, targeted therapy and immunotherapy are used alone or together. The plan depends on cancer type and stage.

Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol, eat plant rich foods, stay active and get vaccinated against HPV and hepatitis B.

Protein rich meals, hydration, gentle activity and rest support strength during treatment. A nutrition plan tailored to your treatment helps.
