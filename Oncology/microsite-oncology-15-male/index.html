Discussing urologic cancer

You can ask to discuss urinary or sexual health concerns privately.

Dr. Yash Sanghvi is a medical oncologist at Arc Urologic Oncology in Ahmedabad. A referral for a prostate, bladder or kidney cancer concern may involve different tests and specialists. Bring the urology note, any tissue result and recent scan reports. The oncologist can discuss how the findings relate to the available options and which questions also need a urologist's input. He welcomes a written question list or a note from your referring doctor. You can ask for an explanation to be repeated and agree who will be in touch about the next step.
